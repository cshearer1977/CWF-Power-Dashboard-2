Sources. Ember yearly electricity data (via the Ember API) is the primary source. Where Ember has no value for a fuel in a given year, the dashboard uses Climate TRACE plant-level activity, summed by country and primary fuel.

Caveats. Climate TRACE models emitting power plants only (coal, gas, oil and biomass), so it cannot fill solar, wind, hydro, nuclear or other renewables, and its plant-level asset data starts in 2021. Its coverage of small and captive plants can be incomplete, so filled values may understate generation. Multi-fuel plants are assigned to their primary (first-listed) fuel; oil and other fossil plants are reported as “Other fossil”. Shares are not shown for years marked *, since their totals are incomplete.

Data refreshed daily by a GitHub Actions workflow.
